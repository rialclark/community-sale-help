Questions

FAQ

Who runs the sale?

I do. I'm Clark Richardson. I coordinate everything remotely, a local partner helps on the ground, and you'll deal with me directly.

Does every home have to take part?

No. Signing up is up to each household. Only the homes that sign up go on the map.

What does our board need to do?

Pick a date, give the go ahead, and help get the word to residents through your usual channels if you can.

Do you work in my area?

I work with neighborhoods anywhere in the US. Planning happens remotely by phone, text, and email, and a local partner helps on the ground. Send a request and tell me where you are.

Is this a site for finding garage sales near me?

No. This is for HOAs and neighborhood groups who want a community sale organized for them.
