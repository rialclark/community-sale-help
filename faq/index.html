HOA garage sale FAQ: answers for boards and residents

The questions boards and residents ask us most about community garage sales. Do not see yours? Call or text (602) 767-0428.

Request a sale Call (602) 767-0428

By Clark Richardson. Last updated November 10, 2026.

Who runs the sale?

We do. We coordinate everything remotely, a local partner helps on the ground, and you deal with us directly.

Do you work in our area?

We work with neighborhoods anywhere in the US. Planning happens by phone, text, and email, and a local partner helps on the ground. Send a request and tell us where you are.

How does a remote sale work?

Planning, signups, the map, flyers, and promotion happen remotely. A local partner handles signs and other legwork on the ground. See how our process works.

What does our board need to do?

Pick a date, give the go ahead, and help get the word to residents through your usual channels if you can.

How far ahead should we plan?

About 8 weeks before the sale date.

Does every home have to take part?

No. Signing up is up to each household. Only the homes that sign up go on the map.

What does it cost?

It depends on your community and what you need. Send a request and we will send you a quote. Our guide to HOA garage sale cost factors explains what goes into the budget.

Do we need a city permit?

It depends on your city or county. Some require a permit for each home and some require nothing. We check your local rules as part of planning, and your board should check your governing documents too.

Can we plan it ourselves?

Yes. Our guide on how to organize an HOA garage sale and our printable checklist walk through it.

Does sending a request commit us to anything?

No. A request just starts the conversation.

Is this a site for finding garage sales nearby?

No. This is for HOAs and neighborhood groups who want a community sale organized for them.

How do we reach you?

Call or text (602) 767-0428, email hello@deklutternow.com, or use the request form on our home page.

Rather have someone run it for you?

We plan and run HOA community garage sales for boards and neighborhood groups anywhere in the US: signups, the map, signs, flyers, and promotion. Sending a request does not commit your HOA to anything.
