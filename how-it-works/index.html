Our process

HOA garage sale organizer: how our process works, near or far

Your board picks the date and gives the go ahead. We take care of the planning from there. Here is what that looks like, step by step, including when your neighborhood is far from us.

Request a sale Call (602) 767-0428

By Clark Richardson. Last updated November 10, 2026.

The short answer: you send a request, we talk through your community and a Saturday about 8 weeks out, and then we handle resident signups, the shared map, flyers, signs, and promotion. Planning happens by phone, text, and email, and a local partner helps with the legwork on the ground. Your board approves the plan and helps spread the word through your usual channels.

The process, step by step

  1. You send a request. Fill out the short form on our home page, or call or text (602) 767-0428. Tell us your community, about how many homes, and a month you have in mind. A request does not commit your HOA to anything.
  2. We talk it through. We get in touch to learn about your community: which streets to include, your board's rules, any city requirements, and what has or has not worked before. We then send you a quote for your sale.
  3. We pick the date together. Usually a Saturday about 8 weeks out, plus a rain date. Your board gives the final go ahead.
  4. We set up signups and the map. We build a short online sign up form for residents and a shared map that fills in as homes join.
  5. We get the word out to residents. We make the resident flyers and the announcements, and your board shares them through the newsletter, email list, or portal.
  6. We promote the sale to shoppers. We post the sale where local shoppers already look and make the shopper flyer and printable map.
  7. Signs and sale day. Signs go up at entrances and corners where your rules allow, the map is shared, and shoppers know where to go.
  8. Wrap up. Signs come down, and we share notes with your board on what to keep or change for next time.

How we run sales remotely

We work with neighborhoods across the US, and most of the organizing does not need anyone standing in your neighborhood. Here is how the work splits:

Done remotely by usDone on the ground
Planning calls, texts, and email with your boardPlacing and removing signs, by our local partner
The resident sign up formOther legwork in the neighborhood, by our local partner
The shared map and the printable mapRunning each home's own sale, by residents
Flyers for residents and shoppersSharing announcements through HOA channels, by your board
Promotion where local shoppers look

You deal with us directly the whole way, by phone, text, or email.

What we need from your board

  • A date and the go ahead.
  • Your community's rules on signs, hours, parking, and common areas.
  • Help getting the word to residents through the channels you already use.
  • One contact person who can answer questions quickly.

What we do not do

  • We do not require every home to take part. Signing up is up to each household.
  • We do not sell items for residents. Each home runs its own sale.
  • We do not skip your rules. If your documents or your city limit signs or require permits, we plan around that.

Questions we hear

Do you work in our area?

We work with neighborhoods anywhere in the US. Send a request and tell us where you are.

Who runs the sale?

We do. We coordinate everything remotely, a local partner helps on the ground, and you deal with us directly.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your community and what you need. Send a request and we will send you a quote. See our guide to HOA garage sale cost factors.

Does sending a request commit us to anything?

No. A request just starts the conversation.
