Printable checklist

HOA garage sale checklist, printable and week by week

Everything your board needs to decide and do, in order. Print it, hand it to your organizer, and tick items off as you go.

Request a sale Call (602) 767-0428

By Clark Richardson. Last updated November 10, 2026.

The short answer: an HOA garage sale needs a board approved date about 8 weeks out, a few ground rules, a resident sign up form, a shared map, signs and promotion, and a plan for leftovers. Print this checklist and tick items off as you go. Rules differ in every HOA and city, so check your governing documents and your city's rules too.

Board decisions

  • Sale date: a Saturday about 8 weeks out
  • Rain date
  • Sale hours
  • Which streets or sections are included
  • Parking guidance for shoppers
  • Whether common areas will be used
  • Where signs can go inside the community, and for how long
  • Who residents contact with questions
  • What happens to leftover items

8 weeks out

  • Board approves the sale, in writing or in the minutes
  • Governing documents checked for any rules on sales, signs, or common areas
  • City or county garage sale rules checked: permits, fees, hours, signs
  • Organizer named

7 to 6 weeks out

  • Resident sign up form built: name, address, email, what they plan to sell
  • Permit applied for, or residents told if each home needs its own
  • Save the date sent through the newsletter, email list, and portal
  • Recruiting flyer left at front doors, never inside mailboxes

5 to 4 weeks out

  • First signup reminder sent
  • Signups counted and shared with the board
  • Signs ordered or made, with a date rider so they can be reused
  • Leftover donation option confirmed

3 weeks out

  • Final signup reminder with the deadline
  • Signups closed at the end of the week
  • Every address added to the shared map
  • Printable map and shopper flyer made

2 weeks out

  • Sale posted in local buy and sell groups, neighborhood groups, and garage sale listing sites
  • Participants sent the sale day details: hours, map link, sign rules, leftover plan
  • Board and management company reminded of the date

Sale week

  • Signs placed, only when your city and HOA allow
  • Map link shared again with residents and shoppers
  • Forecast checked and rain date decision made
  • Printed maps ready

Sale day

  • Map shared early in the morning
  • Signs checked mid morning
  • Someone available by phone or text for questions
  • Signs taken down when the sale ends

After the sale

  • Leftovers donated or picked up
  • Thank you sent to residents
  • Notes saved on what to change next year
  • Next year's date suggested to the board

Questions we hear

Can we print this checklist?

Yes. Use the print button or your browser's print option. The page prints without the menus.

Who should own the checklist?

One organizer, with the board handling the decisions at the top.
